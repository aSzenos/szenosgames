Privacy Policy for RGB Switcher: Color Roguelike

Last Updated: October 16, 2026

Matyas Juhasz, operating under the name Szenos Games ("we", "our", or "us"), operates the mobile game RGB Switcher: Color Roguelike (the "Game").

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use the Game, how that information is used, and the role of third-party services used by the Game.

1. Information We Collect

Game Save Data

Your game progress, settings, and in-game currency are stored locally on your iOS device. We do not operate an online account system or a cloud-save service, and we do not upload your full game-save file to our own servers.

However, certain limited gameplay and purchase-related metrics derived from local game data may be sent to third-party services, as described below. This does not mean that your full local save file is uploaded.

If you delete the Game or remove its local data, your locally stored game progress may be lost.

Information Processed by Third-Party Services

The Game uses third-party services for advertising, gameplay analytics, in-app purchases, and Game Center functionality. These services may process information associated with your device, purchases, or use of the Game in order to provide their services.

The Game uses Apple's Game Center service to provide features such as leaderboards and achievements. Information associated with your Game Center account, including your player identifier, scores, achievements, and related gameplay information, may be processed by Apple to provide these features. Game Center is operated by Apple and is subject to Apple's privacy policy.

In-App Purchases

The Game offers optional in-app purchases of virtual items, such as Prisms, and a Premium package. Apple processes the actual payment transactions through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card number, billing address, or other payment credentials used to complete an App Store purchase.

To provide purchased content and related features, the Game may record whether a purchase occurred, the type of item or package purchased, and an estimated aggregate purchase amount. These purchase-related records may be used to unlock or maintain purchased features and, for players who are not classified as children by the Game's age gate, may be used in gameplay analytics and advertising-segmentation signals as described in this Policy. Apple separately processes transaction information under its own terms and privacy practices.

Gameplay Analytics

The Game uses Unity Analytics, part of Unity Gaming Services, to collect pseudonymous gameplay telemetry associated with a randomly generated, installation-specific identifier. Unity Analytics may also process technical information such as an IP address and an iOS device identifier, as described in Unity's privacy documentation.

The analytics events configured by the Game may include:

  • tutorial progress;
  • run/session information, including the level reached, starting Warp, reason a run ended, and run duration;
  • leagues reached;
  • when the in-game shop or Premium information screens are opened;
  • the types of in-game-currency purchases and real-money purchases; and
  • the category of rewarded advertisement viewed.

These analytics are used to understand gameplay, evaluate progression and game balance, identify issues, and improve the Game. For players identified as children under the applicable age threshold, Unity Analytics collection is disabled as described in Section 3.

For additional information about data processed through Unity services, including Unity Analytics, Unity Ads, ironSource, and Unity LevelPlay, please see the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

2. Advertising – Unity LevelPlay

The Game uses Unity LevelPlay as an advertising mediation service to display advertisements, including optional rewarded advertisements. Through LevelPlay mediation, the Game may use the Unity Ads, ironSource, and Google AdMob advertising networks.

LevelPlay and the advertising networks participating in an ad request may process information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location or region, device and operating-system information, advertising and app interaction information, and information needed to deliver and measure ads and help prevent fraud.

For players who are not classified as children by the Game's age gate, the Game may also send LevelPlay certain advertising-segmentation properties: the app's first-install/creation date (UserCreationDate), the number of completed zones (Level), whether the player has made an in-app purchase (IsPaying), and the estimated total amount spent on in-app purchases (IapTotal). These values are used for audience segmentation and to optimize ad delivery, relevance, and monetization. The Game does not send these custom segmentation properties for players classified as children.

The exact information processed may depend on the advertising network involved, the Game's configuration, and your privacy choices and permissions.

For additional information, please see the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy, the Unity LevelPlay Legal Resources, and Google's Privacy Policy.

Advertising and Privacy Choices on iOS

Because the Game is available only on iOS, Apple's privacy controls apply. Where required, the Game may request permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework before allowing tracking as defined by Apple. You can change this permission through your iOS device settings.

Refusing ATT permission does not necessarily stop all advertising-related data processing. You may still receive non-personalized or contextual advertisements, and advertising providers may process information needed to request, deliver, measure, or protect ad services as permitted by applicable law and their privacy policies.

Age-Based Advertising

The Game includes an age gate that asks the player to select their age when the Game is first launched. The age selected is used to apply age-related privacy and advertising settings based on the applicable threshold for the player's region.

If a player is identified as being below the applicable threshold, the Game is configured to apply child-directed advertising restrictions. Advertisements shown to these players are intended to be non-personalized or contextual and are not intended to use behavioral advertising based on the player's personal information.

For players who are not classified as children by the age gate, personalized advertising may be enabled only where permitted by applicable law and subject to the relevant privacy choices, including ATT permission where required and consent choices where applicable.

The Game is designed for a general audience and is not specifically directed toward children.

Players in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland

For players in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the Game uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to present and manage advertising-related consent messages and choices where required by applicable law. The choices recorded through UMP may be passed to relevant advertising services as supported by the integration.

Google UMP consent choices and Apple's ATT permission address different matters; ATT does not replace consent required under applicable data-protection or electronic-privacy laws. Unity Analytics is a separate service, and its data collection is subject to its own applicable privacy controls and requirements.

You may change your ATT permission through your iOS settings. Where UMP provides privacy options for your region, you may use those options to review or change the relevant consent choices.

For more information about Google's consent requirements, see the Google UMP guidance for EEA users and Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Children's Privacy

The Game uses an age gate when it is first launched. The age gate relies on the age selected by the player and is used to apply age-related restrictions. The Game is designed for a general audience and is not specifically directed toward children.

If a player is identified as under 13, or below a higher applicable age threshold in their region where required, the Game applies age-appropriate advertising restrictions. For such players, the Game disables Unity Analytics telemetry and does not send the custom gameplay or purchase-related advertising-segmentation properties described in Section 2. Advertisements may still be shown, but are intended to be non-personalized or contextual.

The Game does not operate an online account system or store game-save files on our own servers. The restrictions described above do not prevent third-party services from processing information strictly needed to provide their services, subject to their own privacy practices and applicable law.

The privacy requirements applicable to children may vary depending on the player's location and applicable law.

4. How We Use Information

Information processed through the Game and its third-party services may be used for purposes such as:

  • operating, maintaining, and improving the Game;
  • providing advertisements and optional rewarded advertisements;
  • providing Game Center features such as leaderboards and achievements;
  • enabling in-app purchases and providing purchased items and Premium features;
  • analyzing gameplay balance, bugs, player progression, and general game performance;
  • understanding in-game shop use, purchase categories, and rewarded-ad engagement;
  • optimizing advertising delivery and monetization using permitted advertising-segmentation properties for players who are not classified as children;
  • preventing fraud, abuse, and unauthorized activity;
  • complying with legal obligations; and
  • responding to questions or requests sent to us by email.

The full local game-save file remains on your device and is not uploaded to our own servers. However, certain derived gameplay and purchase metrics—such as the number of completed zones, app installation date, whether a purchase has been made, and estimated total purchase amount—may be sent to Unity LevelPlay for advertising segmentation and optimization for players who are not classified as children. Unity Analytics may receive gameplay telemetry as described in Section 1, except when collection is disabled for players classified as children.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We do not maintain an online database containing your full game-save file. Gameplay analytics and advertising-related information processed by third-party providers may be retained according to their policies, legal obligations, and system settings. Unity's published Analytics documentation states that personal data is retained for 13 months by default, although retention may depend on the service and its configuration.

The Game includes a Delete My Data button in the Settings menu. Using this button clears locally stored game data and sends a request to Unity Analytics to delete the associated Analytics data stored on Unity's servers. Unity's documentation says that an SDK data-deletion request initiates a server-side purge and that player data associated with the request is deleted within the next 30 days. If the device is offline, the SDK may retry the request when connectivity is restored.

This deletion request applies to Unity Analytics data handled by that feature. It does not automatically delete information independently processed by Unity LevelPlay, Unity Ads, ironSource, Google AdMob, Apple Game Center, or Apple's App Store. You may need to contact the relevant provider or use its privacy controls for those records. If you continue playing after deletion and Analytics collection is enabled, new analytics data may be collected again where permitted by applicable law.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures appropriate to the nature of the services we operate to protect information that we process directly. Because the Game does not use an online account or server-side save system, we do not maintain a central database containing your full game-save file.

Third-party providers are responsible for the security of information they process through their own services, subject to their respective policies and contractual obligations.
