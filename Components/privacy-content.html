Privacy Policy for RGB Switcher: Color Roguelike

Last Updated: August 25, 2026

Matyas Juhasz, operating under the name Szenos Games ("we", "our", or "us"), operates the mobile game RGB Switcher: Color Roguelike (the "Game").

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use the Game, how that information is used, and the role of third-party services used by the Game.

1. Information We Collect

Game Save Data

Your game progress, settings, and in-game currency are stored locally on your iOS device.

We do not operate an online account system or a cloud save service for the Game, and we do not collect or store your game save data on our own servers.

If you delete the Game or otherwise remove its local data, your locally stored game progress may be lost.

Information Processed by Third-Party Services

The Game uses third-party services for advertising and Game Center functionality. These services may process information associated with your device or use of the Game in order to provide their services.

The Game uses Apple's Game Center service to provide features such as leaderboards and achievements. Information associated with your Game Center account, including your player identifier, scores, achievements, and related gameplay information, may be processed by Apple to provide these features. Game Center is operated by Apple and is subject to Apple's privacy practices.

2. Advertising – Unity LevelPlay

The Game uses Unity LevelPlay, a third-party advertising mediation service, to display advertisements, including optional rewarded advertisements.

LevelPlay and its advertising services may process information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location or region, device and operating-system information, advertising and app interaction information, and information necessary to deliver, measure, prevent fraud related to, and improve advertising.

The exact information processed may depend on the advertising services used and on the privacy choices and permissions available to you.

For additional information about data processed through Unity services, including Unity LevelPlay and advertising services, please see the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

For additional information about LevelPlay privacy and regulatory settings, please see the Unity LevelPlay Legal Resources.

Advertising and Privacy Choices on iOS

Because the Game is available only on iOS, Apple's privacy controls apply.

Where required, the Game may request permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework before allowing tracking as defined by Apple.

You can change your tracking permission at any time through your iOS device settings.

Refusing ATT permission does not necessarily mean that all advertising-related data processing stops. You may still receive advertisements, including non-personalized or contextual advertisements, and third-party advertising services may still process information that is permitted under applicable law and their respective privacy policies.

Age-Based Advertising

The Game includes an age gate that asks the player to select their age when the Game is first launched.

Based on the player's response and the applicable legal requirements, the Game may determine whether the player should be treated as a minor for advertising purposes.

If a player is identified as below the applicable threshold for their region, the Game is configured to treat the player as a minor for advertising purposes and to provide this information to the advertising service where required. Advertisements shown to these players are intended to be non-personalized and are not intended to use behavioral advertising based on the player's personal information.

If a player is identified as an adult, personalized advertising may be enabled where permitted by applicable law and, where required, subject to the player's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) choice.

The Game is designed as a general-audience game and is not specifically directed toward children.

Players in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland

For players located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, advertising-related data processing is subject to applicable privacy and data protection laws.

The Game uses age-based controls and Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework where applicable. These controls do not replace any additional consent that may be required under applicable privacy laws.

Where consent is required for advertising-related data processing, the relevant advertising service may require consent to be provided through its applicable privacy and consent mechanisms.

You may withdraw or change permissions granted through Apple's App Tracking Transparency settings at any time through your iOS device settings. Where a separate consent mechanism is provided or required by the applicable advertising service, consent may also be withdrawn through that mechanism.

3. Children's Privacy

The Game uses an age gate when the Game is first launched.

The Game is designed for a general audience and is not specifically directed toward children.

If a player indicates through the age gate that they are under 13, the Game applies age-appropriate advertising restrictions and does not intentionally provide behavioral or personalized advertising to that player.

We do not knowingly collect children's personal information for the purpose of creating user accounts, maintaining profiles, or providing personalized services.

The Game does not operate an online account system or store children's game progress on our servers.

The specific privacy requirements applicable to a child may vary depending on the player's location and applicable law.

We may use information that is available to us or provided through third-party services for purposes such as:

  • operating and maintaining the Game;
  • providing advertisements and optional rewarded advertisements;
  • providing Game Center features such as leaderboards and achievements;
  • preventing fraud, abuse, and unauthorized activity;
  • complying with legal obligations; and

We take reasonable measures appropriate to the nature of the services we operate to protect information that we process directly.

Third-party providers are responsible for the security of information they process through their own services, subject to their respective policies and contractual obligations.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights regarding personal information processed about you, including rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, or to object to certain processing.

Where information is processed directly by a third-party service provider such as an advertising service or Game Center, you may need to exercise certain rights directly with that provider.

California Residents (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, applicable law may give you additional rights, including the right to know what personal information is collected about you, the right to delete that information, the right to correct inaccurate information, and the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information.

Certain sharing of device or advertising identifiers with advertising partners for advertising purposes may constitute a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information under California law.

You may control Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission and related advertising settings through your iOS device settings. Where applicable, additional privacy choices may also be available through the advertising service's privacy mechanisms.

EEA, UK, and Swiss Residents (GDPR)

If you are located in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal data, to data portability, and to object to processing, including processing based on consent or legitimate interest, subject to the exceptions provided by applicable law.

Where personalized advertising is enabled, you may withdraw or change your App Tracking Transparency permission at any time through your iOS device's tracking settings. Where a separate consent mechanism is required or provided by the applicable advertising service, you may also withdraw consent through that mechanism.

If you have a privacy-related question concerning the Game or information that we process directly, you can contact us using the details below.
